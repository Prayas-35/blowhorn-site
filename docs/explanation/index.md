# Explanation

> Understand why: context and trade-offs, no procedures.


Explanations give the reasoning behind Blowhorn's behaviour: how a post travels from the queue to the platforms, where your data lives, who may post as whom, why Blowhorn uses your own browser, how it paces itself, how it avoids repeat posts, how scheduling works, what runs when the app is closed, what the analytics numbers mean, and how an AI assistant stays inside your rules over MCP.

Read these to build judgment. When you want to act, follow a [how-to guide](/docs/how-to/) instead.
