# Try Blowhorn in your browser

> A hands-on simulation of the real queue. Pick a formation, build a queue, and watch the schedule fill in. Nothing is posted, and nothing leaves your browser.

## How it works

1. **Pick a formation.** Muster, Battalion Map, One Thunder or Call to Arms. Each one changes who sends each post in the schedule.
2. **Build your queue.** Add a sample post, or write your own.
3. **Run the schedule.** Each post takes the next slot, starting tomorrow at 9:00.

This is a simulation. Nothing is posted, and nothing leaves your browser.
