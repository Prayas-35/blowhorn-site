Interactive demo

Try Blowhorn in your browser

A hands-on simulation of the real queue. Pick a formation, build a queue, and watch the schedule fill in. Nothing is posted, and nothing leaves your browser.

  1. 1

    Pick a formation. Four ways to run your fleet.

  2. 2

    Build your queue. Add a sample post, or write your own.

  3. 3

    Run the schedule. Watch each post go out in turn.

Step 1

Pick a formation

Four ways to run your fleet. Each one changes who sends each post in the schedule below.

Muster Battalion Map One Thunder Call to Arms

Muster

Every computer you own is a recruit.

Install Blowhorn on one computer or fifty. Each one enlists, reports for duty and takes orders from you or your AI agent.

In this simulation, each post goes out from the next computer in your roll call.

Battalion Map

Your squad, on one map.

Install Blowhorn on every computer you have. Each one shows up as a unit, reports in and takes the next order.

In this simulation, each post goes out from a unit on your map, reporting in turn.

One Thunder

Many voices. One thunder.

Your teammates, your computers, the brother in arms at the next desk. Major gathers every voice into one call, right on time.

In this simulation, each post goes out from the next voice in your crew.

Call to Arms

From one recruit to a full squad.

Install Blowhorn on one computer, then the next. Bring your team and your agents. Then Major sounds the call.

In this simulation, each post sounds the next beat: Enlist, Muster, Rally, Thunder.
