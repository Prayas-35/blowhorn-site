# Getting the most from your Blowhorn subscription

One thing is metered: voices. Everything else is not. Nineteen ways to lean on that shape, from the General and the rest of the cast.


Blowhorn is free during early access, and paid plans are not set yet. What follows is written for the paid plans as planned, so read it as the shape of the deal to come. The shape is simple, and the General has read it twice, which is once more than necessary.

> **The General:** "One thing is metered. The rest is ground you already own. Take it."

One thing is metered: **voices**. A voice is one name a reader sees as the author. Everything else that other tools charge for is deliberately not metered: not computers, not platforms, not people, not how much you post, not safety, not dry runs, not the command line or MCP, and not approval of your own work. The tips below are all ways of leaning on that shape.

## Voices

{{< major >}}One name, every platform. If you can count to two, you can count your voices.{{< /major >}}

**Count names, not sign-ins.** One voice is one name readers see as the author, and it covers that name on every platform, with one account on each. Your own LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, GitHub and every other account in your name are all one voice: you.

**Add platforms to a voice you already have.** Putting your X account next to your LinkedIn costs nothing. Only a new name, or a second account on a platform you already use, is a new voice.

**Keep your names tidy.** If one X sign-in posts as both you and your company, that is 2 voices, because readers see two names. Give each name its own account only when readers should see two names.

**Budget for Pages, not Groups.** A Company Page is your brand's voice, not yours, and each Showcase Page is a voice of its own. LinkedIn Groups are places you post into, so they never count as voices, even the ones you own.

**Bring every computer.** Computers are never metered on paid plans. One subscription covers as many computers as you like, all sharing one queue, at one flat rate. Install it on every computer you own. (Free covers one Mac.)

**Posting keeps working offline.** Once plans are checked, posting keeps working for up to 72 hours if a computer loses the internet.

## People

  <img src="/assets/brand/cast/keyboard-warrior-base-640.webp" alt="Keyboard Warrior, a gold rooster with a keyboard shield and green keys" width="320" height="180" loading="lazy">

**Bring the whole team.** People are free and unlimited. Teammates who draft, approve, or join launches cost nothing. Only the voices that publish cost.

**Let supporters amplify for free.** A supporter lending their profile to someone else's wave counts against that account's plan, not the supporter's. Amplifier profiles are the cheap way to widen a wave: they only amplify, they never publish.

**Use approvals; they are included.** Approvals across people are part of the team plan, not a separate fee. Other suites gate approvals per seat.

**Post into every group, channel, and subreddit.** Destinations never count, even the ones you run. Groups, subreddits, Slack channels and other places you post into are free.

**Run each client separately.** A workspace inside your own organization is team territory at any size. A workspace run for another organization is a client workspace, priced per client rather than per seat, and client approvers are free guests. Keep clients strictly separate and the roll-up still shows you all of them.

## How much you post

  <img src="/assets/brand/cast/robo-recruit-base-640.webp" alt="Robo-Recruit, a squad of tin-can chickens with wind-up keys, white eyes and blank stares" width="320" height="180" loading="lazy">

**Post as much as you like.** Activity quantity is never a price lever. Only safety caps apply, and they are the same for everyone and never for sale. Going over a plan's voice count never deletes anything; extra voices sit out until you make room.

**Check before you post, free, forever.** Dry runs are never refused and never gated, on any plan. Checking is always free.

**Use the command line, MCP, and your own model on every plan.** The CLI, dry run, JSON output, and agent interface are never gated, and bringing your own model is free on every plan.

**The included model is flat, never credits.** If you use the included model, you pay a flat amount, not per token or per credit, with a stated fair-use amount. Past it, things slow down or fall back to your own model.

## Paying

  <img src="/assets/brand/cast/corporal-clover-base-640.webp" alt="Corporal Clover, a black-and-white cow in a navy cap, writing in her ledger with a pencil" width="320" height="180" loading="lazy">

**Pay yearly when you can.** A year paid up front costs less than the same plan paid month by month.

**Start with the smallest plan that covers the voices you actually have.** For most people that is you and your brand. Add voices one at a time as you grow, instead of jumping to a bigger plan.

**Free is attended only.** On Free every send is approved by hand, one at a time. If you want the scheduler, routines, standing orders, and by-wire sending, the smallest paid plan is the step up.

**A lapsed plan is never a brick.** If a plan lapses, the queue, history, analytics, and every read-only command keep working. The account drops to Free limits, and nothing gets deleted.

That is the whole trick. The meter is voices. Everything else is ground you already own, so use all of it.
