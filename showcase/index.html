What it is

A social console that runs on your computer

You write a post once, queue it, and Blowhorn sends it from your own computer, through your Chrome profiles, at a pace you choose.

Nothing posts from a vendor server. No server signs in as you, and no post goes out without your queue saying so. Your computers coordinate through one queue, so the same post is never sent twice.

Blowhorn is a desktop app with a small browser extension. System requirements has the exact versions it needs.
