# Your voice, on every front

> Blowhorn is a social media console that runs on your own computer. It drives your Chrome profiles through the Blowhorn extension, so every post goes out as you, at a human pace.

## What it is

Blowhorn is a desktop app with a small browser extension. You write a post once, queue it, and Blowhorn sends it from your own computer, through your Chrome profiles, at a pace you choose. Nothing posts from a vendor server.

## Four formations


### Muster

Every computer you own is a recruit. Install Blowhorn on one computer or fifty. Each one enlists, reports for duty and takes orders from you or your AI agent.

### Battalion Map

Your squad, on one map. Install Blowhorn on every computer you have. Each one shows up as a unit, reports in and takes the next order.

### One Thunder

Many voices. One thunder. Your teammates, your computers, the brother in arms at the next desk. Major gathers every voice into one call, right on time.

### Call to Arms

From one recruit to a full squad. Install Blowhorn on one computer, then the next. Bring your team and your agents. Then Major sounds the call.

## What it does


- **It's you, not a bot.** Blowhorn drives the browser you already sign in to. No server logs in with your password, session cookie or 2FA secret.
- **One queue, every computer.** Add computers for coverage. Each action is claimed by one computer, and whichever computer is awake keeps the schedule.
- **A fleet behind every post.** Amplify reposts and comments from every profile you run while the post is still fresh. Teammates joining your fleet is coming soon.
- **Your hand on the switch.** Pause one action, one computer or every computer at once, with the reason recorded. Resume and due times carry forward.
- **Careful by default.** Human-paced typing, built-in caps, one click per run, one automation per platform per profile at a time.
- **Where technical teams talk.** Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack sit in the same queue as LinkedIn, X and Bluesky.

## Where it works
Blowhorn works with LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Reddit, Hacker News, Slack, Bluesky, GitHub, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook.
