Voices

What is a "voice"?

A voice is one name a reader sees as the author. In plain words: you, or a brand you post as. One voice covers that name on every platform, with one account on each, so your own LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, GitHub and every other account in your name are all one voice: you. A second account on the same platform is a second voice. Groups, subreddits, Slack channels and other places you post into don't count, even the ones you run.

How do I count my voices?

Count names, not sign-ins. You plus your company is 2 voices. Add a LinkedIn Showcase Page for your product and it's 3. Running a LinkedIn Group and a subreddit on the side? Still 2. If one X sign-in also posts as your company's account, that's 2 voices, because readers see two names.

Is adding a platform the same as adding a voice?

No. Adding a platform to a voice you already have doesn't add a voice: put your X account next to your LinkedIn and you're still one voice. A new name, or a second account on a platform you already use, is a new voice. Slack is generous: one Slack sign-in can reach several workspaces, and channels never count.

How do LinkedIn Pages and Groups count?

You post to a Company Page or Showcase Page through your own LinkedIn sign-in, inside your Blowhorn profile, so there's no extra login to manage. Counting still goes by the name readers see. A Company Page is your brand's voice, not yours, and each Showcase Page is a voice of its own. LinkedIn Groups are places you post into, so they're free and never count as voices, even the ones you own.

My teammates and I share a brand. How does that count?

The brand is one voice, however many people help with it. A teammate who posts as themselves is their own voice. Nobody hands over a password: Blowhorn never asks for one.

Accounts and computers

What's the difference between a Blowhorn account, a Blowhorn profile and a Chrome profile?

Your Blowhorn account is you as a customer: you sign in at cloud.blowhorn.ai, and it's where a plan lives. A Blowhorn profile holds one sign-in per platform, so a second LinkedIn or X account means a second Blowhorn profile. A Chrome profile belongs to your browser and keeps its saved sign-ins. Blowhorn pairs each Blowhorn profile with a Chrome profile on your computer and posts from there. A Blowhorn profile is usually one voice, but not always: LinkedIn Pages ride along inside the admin's Blowhorn profile and still count as voices of their own.

Do I need a computer?

Yes. Blowhorn runs on your computer, Apple silicon or Intel, together with a small browser extension. An iPad, an iPhone or an Android phone is not a computer for this: Blowhorn posts from a real computer, signed in as you. The good news is that the number of computers does not change the price. One subscription covers as many computers as you like, all sharing one queue, at one flat rate. Bring every computer you own.
