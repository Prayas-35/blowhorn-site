What Blowhorn does for Social media managers

  • One queue for every channel

    LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, GitHub and Slack from one queue, posted as the company and as you.

  • Many profiles, one desk

    The company profile, your profile, the founder's: each its own browser profile, all from one menu bar.

  • A sound wave on launch day

    Schedule one message and have every profile you add amplify it at the moment you pick, in one coordinated blast.

  • Posted as you, never as a bot

    Blowhorn does the typing and clicking in your own browser, signed in as you. Nothing is branded sent via Blowhorn.

The week's posts, drafted and held for you

Your assistant can draft the week's posts from your content calendar. Every write is a dry run until you confirm, and an agent can never approve its own content.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I post as the company or as myself?

Both. Each profile is its own browser profile on your computers, so the company post goes out as the company and your repost goes out as you.

Does Blowhorn log in as me from its servers?

No. It does the typing and clicking in your own browser, signed in as you. No vendor server logs in as you.

What about Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube?

Blowhorn works on them in your own browser, signed in as you, like LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News.

Can my team amplify the launch?

Yes. Teammates and supporters add their own profiles to your account and join the wave. Multi-user team invites: Coming soon.

Not a bot. You, at full volume.
